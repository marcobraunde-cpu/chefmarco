Classic Pad Thai

Authentic Thai stir-fried rice noodles with seared prawns, tofu, and fresh garnishes.

Prep Time
20 Mins
Cook Time
10 Mins
Yield
2 Servings
Skill Level
Intermediate

Video Walkthrough

Ingredients

Pad Thai Sauce

  • 45g Tamarind paste
  • 35g Palm sugar, chopped
  • 30ml Fish sauce
  • 15ml Sriracha sauce

The Stir-Fry

  • 200g Dried rice stick noodles
  • 30ml Vegetable / peanut oil
  • 150g Firm tofu, small dice
  • 10g Garlic, minced
  • 15g Shallot, finely diced
  • 150g Large shrimp (U15)
  • 2 Large eggs
  • 30g Garlic chives, batons
  • 100g Fresh bean sprouts

Garnish & Finish

  • 15g Roasted peanuts, crushed
  • 5g Dried chili flakes
  • 20g Fresh bean sprouts
  • 1 Lime wedge
  • Fresh cilantro sprigs

Preparation Method

  1. Noodle Preparation: Submerge dried rice stick noodles in room-temperature water for 30–40 minutes until pliable yet firm. Drain thoroughly.
  2. Pad Thai Sauce: Combine tamarind paste, palm sugar, and fish sauce in a small saucepan over low heat. Stir continuously until palm sugar dissolves and sauce turns glossy. Set aside.
  3. Sear Proteins: Heat vegetable oil in a carbon-steel wok over high heat. Fry diced tofu until golden brown. Push aside, add shrimp in a single layer, and cook for 1-2 minutes per side until seared. Remove shrimp and reserve.
  4. Sauté Aromatics: Add minced garlic and shallot to the remaining oil in the wok. Stir-fry for 30 seconds until intensely aromatic.
  5. Scramble Egg: Push aromatics to the side, pour beaten eggs into the empty center of the wok, allow to set slightly, then scramble gently.
  6. Combine & Glaze: Add drained noodles and pour the Pad Thai sauce over the top. Toss vigorously over high heat until noodles absorb the sauce and soften (approx. 2 minutes).
  7. Final Fold: Fold in garlic chive batons and bean sprouts. Toss for 15 seconds, remove from heat immediately to retain crunch.

Visual Plating SOP

Ensure exact visual standards for kitchen output:

  • Central Elevation: Twirl noodles tightly using tongs and stack vertically in the center of a shallow ceramic bowl.
  • Crowning Protein: Nest seared shrimp neatly at the top peak, topped with fresh cilantro.
  • Side Garnishes: Place crushed peanuts, dried chili flakes, and raw bean sprouts in distinct, neat mounds along the rim.
  • Finishing Touch: Position a fresh lime wedge opposite the bean sprouts with raw garlic chives scattered cleanly.
